How to use Aspire Copa Board

  1. Pick a format (9v9 or 11v11 for U14; 7v7 optional) and a formation for each team.
  2. ✋ Move: drag any player or the ball with a finger or the mouse.
  3. Pass draws a solid arrow, Run a dashed arrow, Dribble a wavy one. Drag on the pitch to draw.
  4. 🧽 Erase: tap or swipe across an arrow to remove it. ↶ Undo takes back the last change.
  5. Build a play: set up frame 1, tap ＋ Frame, move players to where they go next and repeat. Press ▶ to watch it.
  6. Coach crib is empty templates you fill yourself (saved in this browser). Schedule / logistics says “add your own notes” — nothing is invented.
  7. Save named plays on this device, export JSON to share, or save a PNG picture of the board.

Keys: Space play/pause · ←/→ step · Ctrl+Z undo · M/P/R/D/E tools.
Everything stays on this device. No account and no internet needed.
